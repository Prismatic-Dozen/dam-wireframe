Spillway Structure Parameters

Spillway consists of 64 gates, under-sluices, and concrete bucket dissipator. Total length of spillway is 914 meters. Crest gates are 15.24m x 6.1m radial gates.

Geology & Foundation

Founded on granite, granitic gneisses, and schists. Consolidated foundation grouting with 30m grout curtain wall at upstream side heel.
